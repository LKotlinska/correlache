Who it's for

Sounds familiar?

“My headaches never feel quite the same from one day to the next. I want to see what each kind has in common.”

“I've been training for a while now. Do my headaches show up after certain workouts, or is it all in my head?”

“I want a clear record of every headache, so I have something to show my doctor.”

How it works

Four steps. Your patterns.
  1. 1

    Log a headache in a few taps

    When it started, how bad it was, where it hurt, triggers, symptoms, what helped and any medication you took. Every detail, without the hassle.

  2. 2

    Add your training

    Pick from the exercise library and add your sets: weight and reps, time or distance. The muscles each exercise works are tagged for you.

  3. 3

    Watch your diary grow

    Headaches, workouts and medication sit side by side, week by week. Give it about a month, and your patterns start to show.

  4. 4

    Find your patterns

    See which patterns your headaches resemble, which exercises and muscle groups stand out, and how soon after training they start. Then get a simple idea of what to try leaving out next.
