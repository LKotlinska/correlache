CorrelAche is a headache and workout diary developed as an examination project at YRGO. It investigates whether headaches correlate with specific aspects of physical training: not only whether you exercised, but which muscle groups you trained, the intensity of the session and how long before the headache it took place.

Background

Most headache trackers record exercise as a single yes-or-no trigger, although a heavy strength session and a light run place very different demands on the body. CorrelAche records training in the same detail as headaches, so that the two can be compared in a meaningful way.

Scope and limitations

CorrelAche is a diary that identifies patterns in the data you record. It does not provide diagnoses or medical advice, and only shows correlation, not causation. If your symptoms concern you, consult a doctor. Your diary can serve as a useful basis for that conversation.

Technology

The application is built with Angular and Angular Material, with a NestJS backend and a MySQL database. The exercise library is based on the open-source Exercises-Dataset.
